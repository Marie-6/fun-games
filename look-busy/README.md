# Look Busy 💼

It's 9:00 AM and your soul is already leaving your body. Sneak away from your desk to the coffee machine, the ping pong table and the nap couch to keep your sanity up. If the boss catches you away from your desk, that's a strike. Three strikes and you're fired. Make it to 5:00 PM, then do it all again tomorrow.

**Play:** https://marie-6.github.io/fun-games/look-busy/

## Controls
The whole game uses four keys: **W A S D**.

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Walk |
| Stand on a fun spot | Slack off (earns fun, restores sanity) |
| W, A, S or D | Start, next day, or restart |

On a phone, on-screen W/A/S/D buttons appear instead.

## How it works
- **Sanity** drains while you're not slacking. If it hits zero, you quit.
- **Fun spots** (☕ 🏓 🛋️ 🕹️ 🥤 🍩) give fun points and sanity, then run out and refill after a while. Coffee also makes you walk faster.
- **The boss** walks around with a yellow vision cone. Walls and cubicles block it. Stay in the cone too long and you get a strike and get marched back to your desk.
- **Your desk** (the glowing chair) is always safe.
- **Day 2:** Gary from accounting wanders around and traps you in small talk.
- **Day 3:** HR starts doing walkarounds too. Everyone gets faster each day.

Your best fun score is saved in your browser.

# Off The Table 🐈

You are a cat. The humans bought nice things — put them on the floor, where they belong. Just don't get caught.

**Play:** https://marie-6.github.io/fun-games/off-the-table/
**Slides:** https://marie-6.github.io/fun-games/off-the-table/presentation/ (arrow keys to move, N for speaker notes, F for full screen)

## How to play
- **Paw out** and swipe things off the edge of the table. Chain crashes quickly for combo points.
- **Meow.** It's cute when the humans are watching, suspicious when they're not.
- **Someone's coming.** Pull your paw back and freeze before the door opens. Anything still moving when they look in gets you caught.

## Controls
| Mode | How |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Hold the mouse button to put your paw out and swipe; let go to act innocent. Press **M** to meow. |
| Phone | Scan the QR code on the laptop and use your phone as the controller. Lift your finger to act innocent. No account needed; works on Wi-Fi or mobile data. |
| Webcam hands | Choose your swiping hand (left or right). Hold it at chest height — that's the table — and sweep sideways. Raise or lower it to reach. Pull your hand out of view to act innocent. Open your mouth wide like "meow" to meow. |

**Webcam tips:** sit about an arm's length from the camera, in good light, so both hands fit in the picture. Use Chrome or Safari on a laptop and allow camera access.

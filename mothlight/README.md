# Mothlight 🦋

You are the last lantern in the garden. Your flame burns down every second. Drink the oil drops to keep it lit, and keep away from the moths. The brighter you burn, the faster they come.

**Play:** https://marie-6.github.io/fun-games/mothlight/

## Controls
The whole game uses four keys: **W A S D**.

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Double-tap any of them | Dash. The flash scatters nearby moths (2.5 s cooldown) |
| W, A, S or D | Start or restart |

On a phone, on-screen W/A/S/D buttons appear instead.

## Scoring
Each oil drop is one point and refills some light. Moths drain your light while they touch you. When the light runs out, the game ends. Your best score is saved in your browser.

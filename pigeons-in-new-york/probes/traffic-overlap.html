Probe: do cars overlap?

This page reuses the car-spawning rule from sketch.js. It simulates 2,000 random lanes for 30 seconds each, once with the old rule and once with the new rule, and counts the frames where two cars in the same lane overlap.

Old rule: a new car only checks the car added just before it.
New rule: a new car checks every car in its lane and waits until there's a gap of at least speed × minT.

What it shows: the spawning rule alone produces overlaps (old) or doesn't (new).
What it doesn't show: how the game looks or feels. It doesn't draw anything, and it uses the Hard-mode minimum gap (minT = 1.2) only.

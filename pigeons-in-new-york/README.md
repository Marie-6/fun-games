# Pigeons in New York 🐦

You are a city pigeon. Cross as many New York streets as you can without getting squashed by taxis, buses or people. The pigeon walks on its own and bobs its head like a real one.

**Play:** https://marie-6.github.io/fun-games/pigeons-in-new-york/

## Controls
The whole game uses one button: **Space** (or tap the screen on a phone).

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Press Space | Stop. Press again to walk |
| Hold Space | Fly over cars and people |

## Rules
- Flying uses up your **Wings** bar. Walking refills it, and so do pizza crusts.
- **Buses** are too tall to fly over.
- You have **3 lives**. After a hit, the pigeon blinks and can't be hurt for 2 seconds.
- Bonus lives cost more each time: 10 crusts, then 20 more, then 30 more...
- Wide streets have a safe island in the middle where you can wait.

## Modes
**Easy** (quiet streets), **Medium** (normal day) and **Hard** (rush hour). Each mode keeps its own best score.

## Neighborhoods
12 neighborhoods, each 4 streets long. Every game starts in Midtown and ends at Coney Island. The 10 in between are shuffled and never repeat: Central Park, Chinatown, Times Square, Brooklyn, Washington Square Park, Brooklyn Bridge, Wall Street, Little Italy, Harlem and Rockefeller Center. Each one has its own look and its own traffic, like horse carriages in Central Park or delivery e-bikes in Chinatown.
